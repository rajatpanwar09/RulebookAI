\# Financial Regulations and Student Fee Policy



\## FR-1.1 Tuition Fee Payment



All students enrolled in a regular academic programme must pay the prescribed tuition fee for each semester before the published payment deadline. The university publishes the amount and deadline through the student portal.



A student who has not completed payment by the deadline may have a financial hold placed on their account. A financial hold can restrict registration, examination form submission, access to selected academic services, and issuance of official documents.



Students are responsible for checking the student portal and official university notices for updated fee information.



\## FR-1.2 Payment Methods



Students may pay university fees through the payment methods listed on the official student portal. Where electronic payment is available, students should retain the transaction receipt until the payment is reflected in their student account.



A payment is considered complete only when the university's payment system records the transaction successfully. A bank transaction that remains pending does not by itself establish that the university has received the fee.



\## FR-1.3 Failed Transactions



If a payment is debited from a student's bank account but does not appear in the university payment system, the student must preserve the transaction reference and contact the finance office.



The finance office may request bank confirmation, transaction identifiers, screenshots, or other evidence before correcting the student's account.



Students should not make repeated payments without checking whether an earlier transaction is pending.



\## FR-2.1 Registration Fees



Academic registration must be completed within the registration period published for the relevant semester. Students are expected to complete both academic registration and any associated fee payment requirements.



A student who has not completed registration within the normal registration period may be prevented from selecting courses until the matter is resolved.



\## FR-2.2 Late Registration



Late registration may be permitted where the university has announced a late-registration window. A student using this route may be required to pay a late-registration charge in addition to the normal academic fees.



Late registration is not automatic. Approval may depend on the reason for delay, availability of courses, outstanding financial obligations, and approval from the designated academic authority.



\## FR-2.3 Extended Late Registration



A student who misses the normal registration deadline may submit a late-registration request within ten calendar days after the published deadline.



The request must normally include the student's reason for missing the deadline and any supporting documents required by the registration office.



If approved, the student must pay the normal registration fee together with the applicable late-registration charge before the registration is activated.



The existence of the ten-day application period does not guarantee approval.



\## FR-2.4 Financial Hardship



Students experiencing significant financial difficulty may apply for financial assistance through the student welfare or finance office.



A hardship application should describe the financial circumstances and provide the evidence requested by the university. Submission of an application does not automatically suspend an existing payment deadline unless the university confirms such an arrangement.



\## FR-2.5 Installment Requests



Students may request permission to pay certain approved fees in installments where an installment scheme has been announced.



An installment arrangement is valid only after written or electronic approval from the authorized office. Students must follow the dates specified in the approved installment plan.



\## FR-3.1 Refund Requests



Refund requests must be submitted through the designated university process. The amount refundable depends on the type of fee, date of withdrawal, applicable regulations, and any non-refundable charges.



The university may deduct outstanding amounts from a refund before releasing the balance.



\## FR-3.2 Duplicate Payments



Where a student has accidentally paid the same fee twice, the student should report the duplicate transaction to the finance office.



The university may verify both transactions before approving an adjustment or refund.



\## FR-4.1 Scholarships and Fees



Students receiving scholarships remain responsible for understanding which fees are covered by their scholarship award.



A scholarship award that covers tuition does not necessarily cover examination fees, accommodation, transportation, penalties, or other charges unless the award letter explicitly states otherwise.



\## FR-4.2 Financial Assistance Service Target



The finance office aims to acknowledge complete financial-assistance applications within five working days.



This is a service target rather than a guarantee of approval. Students should allow additional time where an application requires verification from another office.



\## FR-5.1 Missed Fee Deadline



If tuition payment is not recorded by the published payment deadline, the student's registration may be cancelled immediately after the deadline.



Students whose registration is cancelled must contact the designated office to determine whether reinstatement is possible. Reinstatement is not guaranteed and may require additional approval or charges.



\## FR-5.2 Financial Holds



A financial hold may remain active until all outstanding amounts are resolved or an approved payment arrangement is recorded.



Students with an active financial hold may be unable to complete selected administrative processes.



\## FR-5.3 Appeals



A student may submit a financial appeal where the student believes an administrative decision was made incorrectly or where exceptional circumstances affected compliance.



An appeal should include relevant evidence. Submission of an appeal does not automatically reverse the decision being appealed.



\## FR-6.1 Responsibility for Deadlines



The university provides deadlines through official channels, but each student remains responsible for monitoring their account and completing required actions on time.



Failure to receive a reminder message does not normally remove a student's responsibility to comply with a published deadline.



\## FR-6.2 Official Records



Finance-related decisions, approved payment arrangements, refunds, and adjustments should be recorded in the university's official systems.



Informal conversations with individual staff members do not by themselves constitute approval of a financial exception.



\## FR-7.1 Exceptional Financial Circumstances



Where regulations permit discretionary review, an authorized committee or officer may consider exceptional circumstances.



The decision-maker may request supporting evidence and may impose conditions on any approved exception.



\## FR-7.2 Student Communication



Students should use their university email address when communicating with finance and registration offices about official matters.



Students should include their student identification details and relevant transaction or application references when submitting a request.



\## FR-8.1 Record Retention



Students should retain receipts, approval messages, application acknowledgements, and other financial records for the duration recommended by the university.



The finance office may request documentary evidence when investigating payment or registration disputes.



\## FR-8.2 Changes to Financial Regulations



Financial procedures and deadlines may be updated through official university notices.



Where a new notice changes an existing administrative procedure, students should follow the latest officially published instruction unless a specific transition rule applies.
