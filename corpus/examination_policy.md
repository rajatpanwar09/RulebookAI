\# University Examination and Assessment Policy



\## EX-1.1 Examination Framework



University examinations are conducted according to the academic calendar and examination schedules published by the examination office. Students are responsible for checking the examination timetable, venue information, registration requirements, and notices issued before the examination period.



\## EX-1.2 Examination Schedule



The examination office publishes examination dates and times through an official university communication channel. Students should verify the final timetable because changes may be announced after an initial schedule has been published.



\## EX-1.3 Examination Venue



The examination timetable or admit card identifies the venue assigned to a student or course. Students must attend the assigned venue unless the examination office officially changes the venue.



\## EX-1.4 Examination Timing



Students should arrive at the examination venue sufficiently before the scheduled start time to complete identification and seating procedures. Entry after the permitted reporting period may be restricted according to examination-office instructions.



\## EX-1.5 Examination Registration



A student intending to appear in a semester examination must complete examination registration within the announced registration period. Registration is subject to applicable academic, attendance, financial, and administrative requirements.



\## EX-1.6 Registration Verification



Students should verify the courses shown on their examination registration. An incorrect course, missing course, or other registration discrepancy should be reported before the correction deadline.



\## EX-1.7 Examination Eligibility



Eligibility to appear for an examination depends on the requirements applicable to the student's programme and course. These requirements may include attendance, semester registration, academic status, and completion of required administrative processes.



\## EX-1.8 Admit Card



Eligible students receive an examination admit card through the method specified by the examination office. Students should verify their name, identification number, courses, examination dates, and venue information on the admit card.



\## EX-1.9 Admit Card Problems



A student who cannot access an admit card should contact the examination office promptly. The student may be required to resolve an eligibility, registration, fee, or administrative issue before an admit card is issued.



\## EX-2.1 Identification



Students must present the identification document specified by the examination office. The document may be checked before entry and during the examination.



\## EX-2.2 Seating



Students must occupy the seat assigned by examination personnel or indicated by the examination arrangement. Students should not change seats without permission from an invigilator.



\## EX-2.3 Examination Materials



Students may bring only materials expressly permitted by the examination instructions. Permitted materials may vary between courses and examinations.



\## EX-2.4 Writing Materials



Students should bring the writing instruments and other basic materials required for the examination. Borrowing materials during an examination is subject to invigilator permission.



\## EX-2.5 Electronic Devices



Mobile phones, smart watches, wireless communication devices, and other unauthorized electronic devices must not be used during an examination. The examination office may publish additional restrictions for particular examinations.



\## EX-2.6 Personal Belongings



Personal belongings must be stored according to the instructions given at the examination venue. The university may designate an area where bags and other belongings must be placed.



\## EX-2.7 Invigilator Instructions



Students must follow reasonable instructions issued by authorized examination personnel. Refusal to follow examination procedures may be recorded as examination misconduct.



\## EX-2.8 Communication



Students must not communicate with another candidate during an examination except where communication is expressly permitted. Questions about examination administration should be directed to the invigilator.



\## EX-3.1 Question Paper Distribution



Question papers are distributed by authorized examination personnel. Students should wait for instructions before opening or writing on the question paper.



\## EX-3.2 Question Paper Check



Students should check that they have received the correct question paper and that the paper appears complete. A suspected printing or page-order problem should be reported immediately.



\## EX-3.3 Answer Booklet



Students must use the answer booklet or answer medium specified for the examination. Students should follow instructions concerning identification information, page numbering, and additional sheets.



\## EX-3.4 Additional Sheets



Additional answer sheets may be issued by examination personnel when required. Students must follow the procedure for requesting and submitting additional sheets.



\## EX-3.5 Submission



At the end of the examination, students must stop writing when instructed and submit the required answer materials. Students should remain seated until the invigilator permits them to leave.



\## EX-3.6 Examination Duration



Students must follow the duration printed on the examination timetable or question paper. Students may not extend the examination period independently.



\## EX-4.1 Examination Absence



A student who fails to attend an examination is recorded as absent unless an approved exception applies. Absence does not automatically create an entitlement to another examination attempt.



\## EX-4.2 Medical Absence



A student unable to attend an examination because of a medical emergency may submit supporting medical documentation through the prescribed procedure. Approval depends on verification and the authority assigned to review medical absence requests.



\## EX-4.3 Personal Emergencies



Students experiencing a serious unexpected emergency should contact the examination office as soon as reasonably possible. Supporting evidence may be required when requesting consideration under an applicable exception.



\## EX-4.4 Family Events



Routine family celebrations, social events, weddings, travel plans, and similar personal arrangements are not automatically recognized as grounds for examination absence. A student should not assume that such circumstances create an entitlement to a replacement examination.



\## EX-4.5 Travel Problems



Travel delays or transport difficulties do not automatically excuse examination absence. Students should plan travel with sufficient time to reach the examination venue.



\## EX-4.6 Approved Replacement Examination



Where the university approves a recognized examination absence, the student may be permitted to take a replacement examination according to the procedure communicated by the examination office.



\## EX-5.1 Assessment Integrity



Students must complete assessments honestly and independently unless collaboration is expressly permitted. Academic misconduct may affect the assessment result and may also lead to disciplinary proceedings.



\## EX-5.2 Plagiarism



Plagiarism includes presenting another person's work, words, ideas, or materials as one's own without appropriate acknowledgement. Cases may be handled under the university's academic integrity procedure.



\## EX-5.3 Unauthorized Collaboration



Students must not collaborate on an assessment when the assessment instructions require independent work. Unauthorized collaboration may be treated as academic misconduct.



\## EX-5.4 Fabrication



Fabricating experimental results, references, attendance information, research data, or other academic evidence is prohibited. Confirmed cases may be referred to the appropriate academic authority.



\## EX-5.5 Impersonation



A person must not sit an examination on behalf of another student. Impersonation is a serious examination-integrity violation and may be referred for disciplinary action.



\## EX-5.6 Unauthorized Assistance



Students must not obtain unauthorized assistance from another person, electronic device, online service, written material, or communication channel during an examination.



\## EX-5.7 Suspicious Material



If unauthorized material is found with a student during an examination, the invigilator may record the incident and follow the examination misconduct procedure.



\## EX-6.1 Examination Misconduct Report



An invigilator who observes suspected misconduct may prepare an incident report. The report should describe the relevant circumstances and identify materials or evidence collected during the incident.



\## EX-6.2 Student Statement



A student involved in a suspected misconduct incident may be given an opportunity to provide a statement according to the applicable procedure. A statement should address the circumstances relevant to the reported incident.



\## EX-6.3 Investigation



The competent authority may review examination scripts, reports, statements, electronic records, physical materials, or other relevant evidence when investigating suspected misconduct.



\## EX-6.4 Decision



A decision concerning examination misconduct is made by the authority designated under university regulations. The decision may include academic consequences, disciplinary consequences, or both where permitted.
