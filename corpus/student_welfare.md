\# Student Welfare and Support Regulations



\## SW-1.1 Student Support



The university provides academic and non-academic support services intended to help students manage their educational responsibilities. Students may approach the appropriate office for guidance concerning academic planning, financial assistance, counselling, accessibility, residential matters, or other student services.



\## SW-1.2 Student Help Desk



The Student Help Desk provides general information about university procedures and directs students to the office responsible for a particular request. The Help Desk does not replace the authority of an academic department, examination office, finance office, or disciplinary committee.



\## SW-1.3 Counselling Services



Students may voluntarily access counselling and wellbeing services offered by the university. Counselling appointments are handled according to the procedures of the counselling centre. Students requiring emergency assistance should use the emergency contact process published by the university.



\## SW-1.4 Accessibility Support



Students requiring reasonable academic accommodations should contact the accessibility support office and provide appropriate supporting documentation. Approved accommodations may apply to teaching, assessment, examinations, or other academic activities depending on the documented requirement.



\## SW-1.5 Accommodation Review



Accessibility accommodations are reviewed according to the procedures established by the accessibility office. An accommodation recommendation does not automatically change academic learning outcomes or essential course requirements.



\## SW-2.1 Financial Assistance



Eligible students may apply for university financial assistance programmes announced during the academic year. Each programme may specify its own eligibility requirements, application period, supporting documents, and maximum assistance.



\## SW-2.2 Scholarship Applications



Scholarship applications must be submitted using the method specified in the relevant scholarship notice. Applicants are responsible for providing accurate information and submitting required documentation before the application deadline.



\## SW-2.3 Scholarship Selection



Scholarship selection may consider academic performance, financial need, programme requirements, community participation, or other criteria specified in the scholarship announcement. Submission of an application does not guarantee financial assistance.



\## SW-2.4 Scholarship Renewal



A scholarship may be renewed when the student satisfies the renewal conditions published for that scholarship. Renewal conditions may include minimum academic performance, satisfactory progress, conduct requirements, and timely submission of renewal documents.



\## SW-2.5 Scholarship Suspension



Financial assistance may be suspended when a recipient no longer satisfies the applicable eligibility requirements. The university should communicate the reason for suspension and any available review process.



\## SW-2.6 Scholarship Appeal



A student who believes a scholarship decision was made incorrectly may submit an appeal when an appeal mechanism is provided. The appeal must contain the information requested by the scholarship office and must be submitted within the stated period.



\## SW-3.1 Emergency Assistance



The university may provide limited emergency assistance to students facing documented short-term financial difficulty. Emergency assistance is subject to available funds and the eligibility rules of the relevant programme.



\## SW-3.2 Emergency Application



An emergency assistance application should describe the circumstances requiring assistance and include supporting information where requested. The reviewing office may request additional documentation before reaching a decision.



\## SW-3.3 Hardship Support



Students experiencing significant financial hardship may apply for hardship support. Applications are assessed according to the financial-support procedure and may require evidence of income, expenses, family circumstances, or other relevant information.



\## SW-3.4 Hardship Decision



A hardship-support decision may approve full assistance, partial assistance, deferred payment, or another remedy permitted by the applicable programme. The decision should state any conditions attached to the assistance.



\## SW-4.1 Student Complaints



Students may raise complaints about university services through the designated complaint mechanism. A complaint should identify the service or decision concerned and provide sufficient information for the university to understand the issue.



\## SW-4.2 Complaint Acknowledgement



The receiving office should acknowledge a properly submitted complaint according to its administrative procedure. An acknowledgement confirms receipt and does not necessarily indicate that the complaint has been accepted on its merits.



\## SW-4.3 Complaint Review



Complaints are reviewed by the office or authority assigned to the relevant matter. Where appropriate, the reviewing officer may obtain information from university staff, students, or other relevant parties.



\## SW-4.4 Complaint Resolution



The university should communicate the outcome of a complaint through the appropriate channel. Where a further review mechanism exists, the communication should explain how the student may request that review.



\## SW-5.1 Student Organizations



Recognized student organizations may conduct activities in accordance with university regulations. Organizations must comply with requirements concerning registration, finance, safety, use of university facilities, and responsible conduct.



\## SW-5.2 Organization Registration



A student organization seeking recognition must submit the required constitution, membership information, office-bearer details, and other documents requested by the student affairs office.



\## SW-5.3 Organization Activities



Organizations must obtain approval before conducting activities that require university facilities, university funding, external speakers, large gatherings, or other permissions specified by university procedures.



\## SW-5.4 Organization Finance



Recognized student organizations must maintain records of income and expenditure. University-provided funds must be used only for approved organizational purposes and may be subject to financial review.



\## SW-5.5 Organization Events



Events must comply with venue capacity, safety, timing, security, and other requirements communicated by the university. The organizing committee is responsible for following conditions attached to event approval.



\## SW-6.1 Student Identification



Students must carry or possess a valid university identification credential when required by university procedures. Identification may be required for examinations, library services, laboratories, residence facilities, events, or controlled-access areas.



\## SW-6.2 Lost Identification



A student who loses an identification credential should report the loss through the designated university procedure. Replacement may require payment of an applicable administrative charge.



\## SW-6.3 Official Communication



Students should regularly check their official university email account and student portal. Notices sent through recognized university channels may contain deadlines, instructions, or decisions affecting the student's responsibilities.



\## SW-6.4 Change of Contact Information



Students should keep their contact information current in the student information system. Incorrect contact information may prevent the university from successfully delivering administrative communications.



\## SW-7.1 Library Support



Students may use university library services subject to library rules. Library services may include borrowing, digital resources, study spaces, interlibrary services, and research assistance.



\## SW-7.2 Borrowing



Borrowed materials must be returned by the stated due date. Renewal may be available where the item has not been requested by another user and where library rules permit renewal.



\## SW-7.3 Overdue Materials



The library may restrict borrowing privileges when materials remain overdue. Students are responsible for returning or renewing borrowed materials according to library procedures.



\## SW-7.4 Damaged Materials



A student may be required to compensate the university when a borrowed item is lost or damaged beyond normal use. The amount and procedure are determined according to library rules.



\## SW-8.1 Health and Safety



Students must follow university health and safety requirements applicable to laboratories, workshops, field activities, residences, and other controlled environments.



\## SW-8.2 Emergency Procedures



During an emergency, students must follow instructions issued by authorized university personnel. Students should not obstruct emergency response activities or enter restricted areas.



\## SW-8.3 Laboratory Safety



Students participating in laboratory activities must use required protective equipment and follow instructions issued by the responsible instructor or laboratory supervisor.



\## SW-8.4 Reporting Hazards



Students should promptly report dangerous conditions, damaged equipment, spills, or other hazards to the responsible university office or staff member.



\## SW-9.1 Student Conduct



Students are expected to behave respectfully toward other students, staff, faculty, visitors, and members of the university community. Conduct that violates university rules may be referred through the applicable disciplinary process.



\## SW-9.2 Harassment



The university does not permit harassment or threatening conduct within university activities or facilities. Complaints concerning harassment should be submitted through the designated reporting mechanism.



\## SW-9.3 Investigation



Where a complaint requires formal investigation, the university may collect statements, documents, electronic records, or other relevant evidence. Participants in an investigation are expected to cooperate with legitimate requests.



\## SW-9.4 Confidentiality



Information relating to student complaints and investigations should be handled according to applicable university procedures and privacy requirements. Confidentiality does not necessarily prevent disclosure where disclosure is required for investigation, safety, or legal compliance.



\## SW-10.1 Student Services Feedback



The university may collect feedback about student services through surveys, consultations, complaint records, and other mechanisms. Feedback may be used to identify areas for improvement.



\## SW-10.2 Service Availability



Student services operate according to published working hours and available staffing. During holidays or exceptional circumstances, service availability may change.



\## SW-10.3 Service Requests



Students should provide complete information when submitting a service request. Incomplete requests may require clarification before the responsible office can process them.



\## SW-10.4 Record Keeping



Student service offices may maintain records necessary to process requests, provide services, demonstrate compliance, and administer university programmes. Records are handled according to applicable institutional requirements.



\## SW-11.1 Student Representation



Students may participate in recognized student representative bodies where such bodies exist. Representative bodies may communicate student concerns to university authorities through established channels.



\## SW-11.2 Student Elections



Where student elections are conducted, candidates and voters must follow the election rules published for the relevant election. Election-related misconduct may result in removal from the process or disciplinary action.



\## SW-11.3 Representation Meetings



Recognized student representatives may meet university officials according to established procedures. Meetings should focus on matters within the authority of the participating representatives and officials.



\## SW-12.1 General Support Principle



Student support policies are intended to provide reasonable access to university services while preserving academic standards and institutional procedures. Support does not automatically create an exemption from an academic or administrative requirement unless an applicable rule expressly provides such an exemption.
