\# Student Residence Hall Handbook



\## RH-1.1 Residence Eligibility



Students may apply for university residence accommodation according to the eligibility criteria published for their academic programme.



Allocation of a room does not guarantee accommodation for the entire degree programme. Students must complete the required renewal process for subsequent academic periods.



\## RH-1.2 Room Allocation



Rooms are allocated by the residence administration according to availability, eligibility, and the applicable allocation procedure.



Students may submit a room preference, but a preference does not guarantee a particular room, floor, building, or roommate.



\## RH-1.3 Check-In



Students receiving a residence allocation must complete check-in within the period specified by the residence administration.



At check-in, students may be required to provide identification and complete an inventory or room-condition form.



Students should report existing damage before occupying the room fully. Damage not recorded during the inspection may be attributed to the occupant subject to the applicable review process.



\## RH-2.1 Residence Fees



Residence fees must be paid according to the published accommodation schedule.



Students who fail to pay required residence charges may receive a financial or accommodation hold.



Accommodation charges are separate from tuition unless an official fee schedule states otherwise.



\## RH-2.2 Security Deposit



A refundable security deposit may be collected when a student accepts a residence allocation.



The deposit may be used to cover verified damage, missing university property, or other charges permitted by the residence agreement.



Any remaining eligible balance is returned according to the university refund procedure.



\## RH-2.3 Visitors



Visitors must comply with residence security procedures.



A resident may be required to register a visitor at the residence reception or security desk.



Visitors may be restricted from entering certain areas or staying overnight where the residence rules prohibit such activity.



Residents are responsible for ensuring that their visitors follow residence rules while on university premises.



\## RH-3.1 Quiet Hours



Residents should maintain reasonable noise levels and respect other students' ability to study and sleep.



The residence administration may establish specific quiet periods during examinations or other academic periods.



Repeated complaints about excessive noise may result in disciplinary review.



\## RH-3.2 Common Areas



Common rooms, study spaces, kitchens, recreation areas, and other shared facilities must be kept clean after use.



Students must not remove university-owned furniture or equipment from common areas without authorization.



Damage to shared facilities should be reported promptly to residence staff.



\## RH-3.3 Cleanliness



Residents are responsible for maintaining reasonable cleanliness in their assigned rooms.



Residence staff may conduct inspections according to the published residence procedure.



Students may be asked to correct cleanliness or maintenance problems identified during an inspection.



\## RH-4.1 Prohibited Conduct



Residents must comply with university disciplinary regulations while living in residence.



Conduct that threatens the safety, security, or reasonable use of accommodation by other residents may result in disciplinary action.



Students must not intentionally damage university property or interfere with residence security systems.



\## RH-4.2 Safety Equipment



Fire extinguishers, alarms, emergency exits, smoke detectors, and other safety equipment must not be tampered with.



Blocking an emergency exit or disabling a safety device is prohibited.



Students should immediately report malfunctioning safety equipment to residence staff.



\## RH-4.3 Emergency Situations



In an emergency, residents should follow instructions issued by residence staff, security personnel, or emergency services.



Residents should familiarize themselves with emergency exits and assembly areas for their building.



\## RH-5.1 Maintenance Requests



Students may report maintenance problems through the residence maintenance system or designated residence office.



Requests should include the room number, nature of the problem, and any information that may help maintenance staff locate the issue.



Urgent safety problems should be reported immediately rather than waiting for a routine maintenance visit.



\## RH-5.2 Room Changes



A student who wishes to change rooms may submit a request to residence administration.



Room changes depend on availability and approval. Students should not move to another room without authorization.



An approved room change may require completion of a new inventory or room-condition record.



\## RH-5.3 Lost Property



Lost property found in residence facilities should be handed to the designated residence office or security desk.



Students searching for lost property should provide a description and relevant location information.



The university does not guarantee recovery of personal property.



\## RH-6.1 Study Facilities



Residence study facilities are intended primarily for academic work.



Students using study areas must respect other users and comply with posted opening hours and facility rules.



\## RH-6.2 Internet Services



Residence internet services are provided for reasonable academic and personal use.



Students must comply with university information-technology policies when using residence networks.



Network access may be restricted where security or maintenance requires it.



\## RH-7.1 Leave and Temporary Absence



Students temporarily leaving residence should follow any sign-out or absence procedure applicable to their building.



Students are responsible for securing their room before leaving.



\## RH-7.2 Checkout



At the end of the accommodation period, residents must complete the checkout process by the date communicated by residence administration.



Students should return keys, access cards, and university property assigned to them.



Failure to complete checkout requirements may result in additional charges where permitted by the residence agreement.



\## RH-8.1 Complaints



Residents may raise accommodation concerns through the designated residence complaint procedure.



Complaints should contain sufficient information for the residence administration to investigate the matter.



Students should normally attempt to resolve routine accommodation issues through the residence office before escalating them.



\## RH-8.2 Residence Administration



Residence administration may issue operational notices concerning maintenance, safety, examinations, holidays, room allocation, and other residence matters.



Students are responsible for reading official residence communications and following applicable instructions.



\## RH-8.3 Changes to Residence Rules



Residence procedures may be updated when operational, safety, or administrative requirements change.



The latest officially published residence notice should be followed where it replaces an earlier operational instruction.
